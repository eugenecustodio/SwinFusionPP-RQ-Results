Amendments after test scoring. The stratified cluster resampling (clusters with and without fake videos drawn separately) and the permutation test with 5,000 permutations were specified on September 30, 2026, before test scoring. Four changes were made after test scoring; none altered a model, threshold, or test set. First, the predeclared permutation test exchanged raw ensemble logits between models with different logit scales, which violated exchangeability. It was replaced by a cluster-level adaptation of the rank-based approach of Bandos et al. (2005), which ranks each model’s video scores before the exchange. The correction changed 10 of the 65 RQ1 and RQ6 decisions; among the 26 architecture comparisons, it removed one significant difference favoring SwinFusion++ and added two. Original values are in Appendix J. Holm decisions from the stored paired-bootstrap p-values matched those of the corrected test in all 73 paired comparisons, including eight RQ4 control comparisons. Second, the RQ1 and RQ6 comparisons were regrouped after scoring into six Holm families, while the RQ4 control families were unchanged: fixed-recipe architecture comparisons within datasets (m = 8) and under frozen transfer (m = 18), configured-system comparisons (m = 8 and 18), and optimization-effect comparisons (m = 4 and 9). The architecture conclusions were identical under the partition recorded before scoring and under one pooled family; only the optimization effect within Celeb-DF v2 depended on the grouping (Holm-adjusted p = 0.021 versus 0.077). Third, the interim decision to present the BO-selected configuration as primary was reverted to the approved plan (Chapter 4). Fourth, supplementary analyses without p-values were added: paired intervals for threshold-metric differences, minimum detectable differences, cross-seed sign agreement, re-analyses with 175 DeeperForensics-1.0 lineage clusters and without each Celeb-DF v2 identity cluster, jackknife-to-bootstrap standard-error ratios, and point means of transfer ROC-AUC. McNemar’s test was not used because every test set contained clusters of several related videos, so one independent decision per cluster could not be assumed.
