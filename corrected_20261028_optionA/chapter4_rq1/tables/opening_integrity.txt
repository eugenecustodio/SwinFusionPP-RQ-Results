Three features limit how cleanly the test sets are separated from model development. First, most FaceForensics++ and DeeperForensics-1.0 test videos share source lineage with another condition’s training or validation videos: 260 of 280 in each FaceForensics++ set and 382 of 402 in DeeperForensics-1.0. The Celeb-DF v2 test set has no recorded cross-condition overlap. Table 1 gives these counts. Second, the preprocessing recovery rules were developed on 247 pooled training videos, 67 of which share lineage with held-out videos (15 physically identical). In addition, frames from 157 held-out videos were shown, without labels or scores, during face-quality review. No claim of untouched preprocessing selection is therefore made. Third, a metadata-only audit before training found that raw file metadata separated real from fake videos in Celeb-DF v2 (validation AUC 0.67) and DeeperForensics-1.0 (0.68). The models were then restricted to the rebuilt face-clip cache, and the repeated audit gave 0.50 on every dataset.
