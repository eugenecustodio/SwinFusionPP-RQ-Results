[NEW] ## Research Question 2: SwinFusion++ Compared With Established Baselines
[NEW] RQ2 asked: What is the performance of SwinFusion++ relative to established baselines when evaluated on the Deepfake subset of FaceForensics++ (HQ/LQ), Celeb-DF datasets and DeeperForensics-1.0? Three kinds of evidence are kept separate. The first is the held-out performance of SwinFusion++; following the analysis plan, the BO-selected configuration is reported with the fixed-recipe result alongside. The second is the Xception baseline that the authors trained in their preliminary study (Custodio et al., 2026), the only established baseline that this group has run on these datasets; it used an earlier pipeline, a single seed, and partly different test populations. The third consists of previously reported results for Xception (Chollet, 2017) and I3D (Carreira &amp; Zisserman, 2017), as evaluated by X. Li et al. (2020), Gu et al. (2021), and Jiang et al. (2020), and for STIL (Gu et al., 2021), FTCN (Zheng et al., 2021), TALL-Swin (Xu et al., 2023), and SFormer (Kingra et al., 2024). No baseline was retrained in the present study, so none of these comparisons is paired and none is tested; they are descriptive.
[NEW] Two features of this study’s test sets further limit comparison with published values. The Celeb-DF v2 test set contains 978 videos split by target identity rather than the official 518-video test list, and the DeeperForensics-1.0 test set is this study’s binary construction of unperturbed manipulated videos and their FaceForensics++ source originals, which differs from the published benchmark settings. Published results also come from their own splits, frame sampling, face crops, and operating points.

[NEW] On FaceForensics++, where both studies used test sets of 280 videos (140 fake) drawn under the same source-pair split rule, SwinFusion++ reached ROC-AUC 0.9964 on C23 and 0.9941 on C40 (fixed recipe 0.9942 and 0.9878), against 0.7725 and 0.8109 for Xception. Its accuracy was 98.21% and 96.07% (fixed recipe 97.86% and 94.64%), against 69.64% and 74.29%. On Celeb-DF v2, SwinFusion++ reached ROC-AUC 0.9983 (fixed recipe 0.9951) on its 978-video test set, while Xception reached 0.6480 on its 859-video test set and labeled every video fake, so its 84.17% accuracy equals the share of fake videos. On DeeperForensics-1.0, both ranked videos almost perfectly (SwinFusion++ 0.9954, fixed recipe 0.9974; Xception 0.9978), but on very different populations: Xception’s test set held 2,385 videos, 91.61% of them fake and including perturbed variants, and at its threshold it missed 1,288 fake videos (accuracy 46.00%).
[NEW] SwinFusion++ therefore ranked videos far better than the preliminary-study Xception on FaceForensics++ C23 and C40 and much better on Celeb-DF v2, although on a different test population; on DeeperForensics-1.0 the two were similar. The two models differ in pipeline, training budget, pretraining, capacity, and number of seeds, so the comparison shows that SwinFusion++ exceeds this particular baseline on FaceForensics++ and Celeb-DF v2, not that the architecture alone accounts for the gap.

[NEW] Answer to Research Question 2
[NEW] On the FaceForensics++ DeepFakes C23 and C40 subsets, Celeb-DF v2, and the DeeperForensics-1.0 construction, the BO-selected SwinFusion++ reached ROC-AUC of 0.9941 to 0.9983 and accuracy of 96.07% to 98.51% (fixed recipe 0.9878 to 0.9974 and 94.64% to 98.76%). It ranked videos far better than the Xception baseline of the preliminary study on FaceForensics++ and Celeb-DF v2 and similarly on DeeperForensics-1.0, a comparison limited by the different pipeline and, for the last two datasets, by different test populations. Against previously reported results, its ROC-AUC was close to or above the listed values on FaceForensics++, and its accuracy on the balanced FaceForensics++ test sets was 1.79 to 2.14 percentage points below the highest reported values (fixed recipe 2.14 to 3.57). Because those results come from other splits and protocols, they place SwinFusion++ within the range of the listed methods on FaceForensics++ but cannot rank it against them.
